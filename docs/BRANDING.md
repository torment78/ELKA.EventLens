# ELKA EventLens Branding

EventLens is part of the ELKA tools family. Its header borrows the circuit-trace composition of ELKA Power Throttle Control while preserving EventLens's charcoal-teal, mint, and amber palette.

## Family Motifs

- Tools and system utilities: circuit-board traces, angular routing, terminal pads, and signal highlights.
- Audio apps and audio plugins: waves and waveforms.
- Hybrid tool/audio products, including plugin hosts: a deliberate combination of both motifs.

The motif identifies the category. Color identifies the product and need not match Power Throttle Control's amber artwork.

The dimensional icon sits beside a prominent ELKA name, EventLens title, and small "by ElkaSoft" signature. Circuit traces frame the banner and foreground. The diagnostic pulse inside the approved icon remains part of its identity, not an audio-family background motif.

The compressed header is used in the project README. The vertical card and approved icon are used in the dark installer wizard, following Power Throttle Control's presentation. Desktop working areas keep the existing compact layout and theme controls.
